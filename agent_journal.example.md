# 📝 Robinhood Agentic Trading | AI Portfolio Journal (Template)

This is an example structure of the local, git-ignored `agent_journal.md` file generated automatically by the AI portfolio manager runtime.

---

## Entry Date: YYYY-MM-DD (HH:MM EDT) — Strategy Execution Summary

* **Market Condition**: High-level summary of market session metrics (S&P 500, Nasdaq, sector volatility).
* **Asset Analysis**:
  * **NVDA**: Technical indicators (1-hour RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands) & PE valuation metrics.
  * **QQQ / SPY**: Index ETF momentum & oversold dip-buying signals.
  * **Options Bucket**: Level 2 Single-Leg ITM Call option setup review.
  * **Crypto Watchlist**: 24/7 momentum scan (BTC, ETH, SOL, DOGE).
* **Investment Decision**: Details of executed trades or pre-trade simulation reviews (`order_checks: {}`).
* **Portfolio State**: Net worth, cash balance, and asset holdings breakdown.
